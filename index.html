How to check a listing

  1. Match the item

    Use the title and item ID to verify you’re looking at the same listing. Check the selected color, size, and price on the current product page.

  2. Compare measurements and photos

    Measure a similar item you own and compare it with the seller’s size chart. If QC photos are available, check the size tag, seams, and visible condition before approving shipment.

  3. Estimate the delivered price

    Add the item price, domestic delivery, agent fees, international shipping, and any applicable taxes. Check the shipping quote again once the parcel weight and dimensions are available.

Frequently asked questions

What is the HipoBuy spreadsheet?

It’s a searchable product directory with HipoBuyFind item IDs and reference prices. Use it to compare listings and build a shortlist before checking current options on HipoBuyFind.

Why are there prices in two currencies?

The catalog shows the listed amount in Chinese yuan and HipoBuyFind’s approximate US-dollar equivalent. Your payment provider may use a different exchange rate.

How do I find a product if its listing changes?

Keep the item ID and search for the exact product title on HipoBuyFind. If several listings have similar titles, verify the item ID, images, and variant before ordering.

What are QC photos?

Quality-control photos show the item received for inspection. When available, they help you check details such as color, measurements, labels, and visible defects before international shipment.
